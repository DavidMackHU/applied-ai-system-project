# Activity Guides

Each section describes what music usually fits an activity or situation. When a listener mentions an activity but not a genre, mood, or energy level, the agent retrieves the matching guide and uses it to fill those gaps.

## studying
Aliases: study, studying, homework, exam, exams, finals, reading, revision
Energy: 0.25-0.45
Moods: focused, chill
Genres: lofi 0.9, ambient 0.7, classical 0.7, jazz 0.5
Instrumental: preferred
Steady, low-distraction background music. Lyrics compete with reading, so instrumental tracks work best for homework and exam prep.

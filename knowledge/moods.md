# Mood Families

Each section is a mood. `Aliases` are words people use for that mood. `Related moods` give partial credit to nearby moods, so a "sad" listener can still be matched with "melancholic" songs.

## happy
Aliases: cheerful, joyful, upbeat mood, feel good, feel-good, good mood
Related moods: euphoric 0.7, uplifting 0.8, confident 0.4, festive 0.5
Energy: 0.6-0.85
Bright, positive songs with major keys and bouncy rhythms.

## euphoric
Aliases: ecstatic, hyped, on top of the world
Related moods: happy 0.7, uplifting 0.6, intense 0.4
Energy: 0.8-1.0
Peak-of-the-night energy. Big drops and hands-in-the-air choruses.

## uplifting
Aliases: inspiring, hopeful, motivating
Related moods: happy 0.8, euphoric 0.5, confident 0.5
Energy: 0.55-0.8
Songs that lift you up and make you feel hopeful.

## chill
Aliases: chilled, laid back, laid-back, mellow mood, cozy
Related moods: relaxed 0.9, focused 0.6, peaceful 0.7
Energy: 0.25-0.5
Easygoing background music that keeps things calm.

## relaxed
Aliases: unwind, unwinding, easygoing
Related moods: chill 0.9, peaceful 0.7, romantic 0.3
Energy: 0.3-0.55
Comfortable, warm, unhurried songs.

## focused
Aliases: focus, concentration, concentrate, productive
Related moods: chill 0.6, relaxed 0.4, peaceful 0.4
Energy: 0.3-0.5
Steady music with few distractions, often instrumental.

## sad
Aliases: heartbroken, melancholy, blue
Related moods: melancholic 0.9, nostalgic 0.4, moody 0.4
Energy: 0.2-0.5
Slow, emotional songs for hard days.

## melancholic
Aliases: bittersweet, wistful
Related moods: sad 0.9, nostalgic 0.5, moody 0.5
Energy: 0.15-0.45
Reflective sadness with a touch of beauty.

## nostalgic
Aliases: throwback, retro, memories
Related moods: moody 0.5, melancholic 0.4, happy 0.3
Energy: 0.4-0.7
Songs that feel like old memories and past decades.

## moody
Aliases: brooding, atmospheric mood, dark mood
Related moods: nostalgic 0.5, melancholic 0.5, intense 0.3
Energy: 0.5-0.75
Shadowy, late-night songs with tension.

## intense
Aliases: aggressive, powerful, hard-hitting
Related moods: angry 0.7, confident 0.5, euphoric 0.3
Energy: 0.8-1.0
Loud, driving, high-adrenaline songs.

## angry
Aliases: mad, furious, rage, pissed off
Related moods: intense 0.8, rebellious 0.6
Energy: 0.85-1.0
Raw, aggressive songs for letting off steam.

## confident
Aliases: swagger, boss, unstoppable
Related moods: intense 0.5, happy 0.4, uplifting 0.5
Energy: 0.65-0.9
Strut-down-the-street songs.

## romantic
Aliases: in love, sensual, lovey
Related moods: relaxed 0.4, happy 0.3, chill 0.3
Energy: 0.4-0.7
Warm, intimate songs about love.
